# Octagon Spire 🥊

An MMA-themed card battler inspired by Slay the Spire. Fight your way from amateur to champion in the octagon!

## Game Overview

**Octagon Spire** is a strategic card game where you play as an aspiring MMA fighter climbing the ranks. Each fight presents tactical decisions as you choose which cards to play, building your deck as you progress toward the championship.

## Key Features

- **Card-Based Combat**: Strategic gameplay with energy management
- **MMA-Themed Moves**: Stand-up strikes, takedowns, and defensive techniques
- **Fighting Styles**: Face different opponent types (Strikers, Grapplers, Technical, Champions)
- **Deck Building**: Earn new cards after each victory to strengthen your arsenal
- **Linear Progression**: 5 fights from amateur to champion
- **Type Effectiveness**: Stand-up vs Takedown mechanics with strategic depth

## How to Play

1. **Select Cards**: Choose which cards to play from your hand (costs energy)
2. **End Turn**: Execute your selected moves, then face your opponent's attack
3. **Win Fights**: Defeat opponents to earn new cards for your deck
4. **Advance**: Progress through 5 increasingly difficult fights

### Card Types

- **🥊 Stand-up**: Striking moves (punches, kicks, knees)
- **🤼 Takedown**: Grappling moves (takedowns, throws, submissions)  
- **⚡ Both**: Versatile moves that work in any position
- **🛡️ Neither**: Utility cards (blocks, healing, energy gain)

### Fighting Styles

- **Strikers**: Strong against grapplers, weak to takedowns
- **Grapplers**: Strong against strikers, weak to stand-up
- **Technical**: Balanced fighters with consistent moves
- **Champions**: Elite opponents with powerful abilities

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in a web browser to start playing! The game is fully self-contained and runs locally.
